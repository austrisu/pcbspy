Reverse-engineer a PCB
from photographs

PCBSpy is a free, in-browser workbench for hardware reverse engineering. Stack and align board scans, annotate pads, vias and holes, trace signals into nets, and overlay KiCad footprints — all running locally in your browser.

Try it out → View on GitHub No install, no account, no upload — your images never leave the browser.

What it does

A focused toolkit for PCB tracing and signal tracing — turning board photos into documented, retraceable electrical structure.

≣

Layered board scans

Import top/bottom photos as non-destructive layers you can move, scale, rotate, flip and freeform-warp for perspective correction.

⧉

Landmark alignment

Co-register top and bottom scans with a least-squares homography fit from matching feature pairs.

◎

Electrical annotation

Drop pads, vias, holes, ground and Vcc points — each carrying a board side (top, bottom, or through).

⟋

Net tracing

Wire dots into nets with a snapping line tool. Through-vias bridge sides; guard rails block cross-net mistakes.

▦

KiCad footprints

Overlay simplified KiCad footprints as adjustable layers to match components against the board.

💾

Save as a .zip

The whole project — images, layers, annotations and nets — saves and reloads as a single portable zip.

See it in action

Three core workflows for retracing a board from images.

Layer alignment

Align top & bottom

Place matching landmark pairs across two scans and compute a homography that snaps both sides into the same world coordinates.

  • Pick a fixed and a moving layer
  • Place 5–8 feature pairs
  • Least-squares co-registration
Annotation & tracing

Annotate & trace nets

Mark pads, vias, holes and power points, then wire them into named nets with a snapping line tool — the heart of signal tracing.

  • Side-aware pads, vias & holes
  • Snapping polyline net tool
  • Color-by-net & hover highlight
Layer visual adjustment

Enhance the scan

Push brightness, contrast, saturation, hue and sharpening per layer to pull faint traces out of a noisy board photo.

  • Per-layer, non-destructive
  • Opacity blending between sides
  • Reset any time

Start retracing a board

Open the tool and load the built-in demo, or drop in your own board photos.
